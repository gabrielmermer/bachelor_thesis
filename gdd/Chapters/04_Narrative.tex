\section{Story Premise}

Due to a immerse drown followed by extreme weather the freshly built 
Polish nuclear reactor imploded in the year 2028. The country being 
in a very tense point just before due to it's tentions on the east border
got into the state of panic, since Chernobyl is more than a mere memory for
most of the population. This has led to massive exodus from the nation, which
led to further instability. During that time further destabilisation from the country
enemies successed in a form of assasinating the prime minister. This in turn made the goverment
evacute into western teritory. The west part of the country has in turn been masively abandoned
by the populus, the military got pulled back to the western part of the country, creating a lawless zone.
The enemies of the state did not launch a full on invasion due to severe radiation in this part of the country,
but small militia units can be found scaveging around.

People who decided to remain are trapped in a zone with little to no help

\section{Characters}

\subsection{Main Player}

The main character that the player controls is a well prepared survivor. He decided that he won't retreat 
due to his want to protect his brother.

\subsection{Brother}

Sibling of the Main Player. He used to be a camera operator for a local TV station before the collapse. 
He decided that he has to record and somehow show to the world as much as he can about this crisis.

\subsection{Scavengers}

Lawless criminals who are known to roam around the shopping mall. They are known to kill, and don't respect 
any form of humanitarian laws. They do what they have to to survive by the means of domination. Despite their 
motivations they allow people to live in some parts of the shopping mall, since they lack the manpower and resources
to fully cleanse the place of human presence. 

\subsection{Shopping Mall civilians}

These are some people that decided to seek shelter in the shopping mall given that it offers many resources helpful for survival.
It's not known how many of them there is.
